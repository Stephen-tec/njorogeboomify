Why I Love Kanye West

Where I discovered him: Radio

When: November 2021

First track: Runaway (feat. Pusha T)

Kanye's production is revolutionary. He samples everything from soul records to orchestral pieces and creates something entirely new. His ability to make club bangers, introspective ballads, and experimental tracks with equal mastery is unmatched. Beyond the beats, his lyrics touch on personal struggles, ambition, and social commentary. He's not just an artist — he's a visionary who has influenced hip-hop and popular culture.
